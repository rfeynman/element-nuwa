# Remaining-metal binary reaction audit

Candidate `metal-binary-additions.json`, version `2026.11.20-metal-audit.1`. Prepared 20 November 2026 UTC. **150 additional equations over 145 distinct elemental pairs**, all 145 pairs absent from the 223-entry baseline. The candidate has 194 referenced substances and 44 sources, comprising 51 modeled scenarios and 99 equation-only records. This is a standalone integration candidate, not a public snapshot or a universal prediction engine.

## Coverage and source evidence

The air and halogen reaction sections of 38 WebElements pages were downloaded and inspected: Ga, In, Tl, Y, Zr, Nb, Mo, Tc, Ru, Rh, Pd, Ag, Cd, Sb, Te, Hf, Ta, W, Re, Os, Ir, Pt, Au, Hg, La, Ce, Pr, Nd, Sm, Eu, Gd, Tb, Dy, Ho, Er, Tm, Yb, and Lu. Existing AgF2 coverage was not duplicated; Ga/In pages supply no equations. Four additional explicit textbook equations cover Cd+Cl2, liquid Ga+Br2, and the SnCl2/SnCl4 endpoints in OpenStax Chemistry 2e §18.11. Lead owns aluminum and further Ga/In oxide sourcing.

Local `raw/metal-binary/manifest.json` records 46 successful raw downloads (38 element pages plus independent phase/review evidence), two browser-rendered factual field extracts, one copied prior OpenStax evidence file, and three failed direct downloads. Failed direct downloads are not presented as downloaded HTML; the Pr/Tb oxide field extracts explicitly identify their different evidence kind. Every retained listed artifact has a SHA-256 checksum. PDF-to-text files are convenience extracts of retained PDFs. Original source pages, manufacturer documents and artwork are not app assets.

Each scenario preserves the source equation and section locator. URLs and retrieval dates accompany the records. WebElements edition dates are unavailable rather than invented. Source prose contains multiple typos; direct formulas were never generated by valence rules.

## Corrections and corroboration

`raw/metal-binary/audit-details.json` records 29 normalization events and 18 withheld/complex source rows. Integer coefficient corrections are mathematical conservation fixes for an explicitly named product: examples include PdO, TeO2, Tl2O and missing Nb/Ta product coefficients. The lowercase `sb2O3` token is corrected to Sb2O3 from the same page’s explicit antimony trioxide prose. The MoCl5 equation is retained; the source prose mistakenly calls that chlorine product a fluoride. No new product was inferred from these typos.

- **HfCl4, ZrCl4, NbCl5 and TaCl5:** the source’s `(l)` product labels are corrected to isolated solids after cooling. Primary producer SDSs (Gelest, ATI and Thermo Fisher) and NOAA CAMEO independently corroborate the solid states. These extra source links are attached to each corrected record. Heating is explicit in the source prose, and the product-after-cooling convention is in the condition label and explanation.
- **Rare-earth oxides:** the WebElements equations omit all phase labels. The page’s halogen equations explicitly identify the bulk metals as solids. NBS NSRDS 49, *Transition Metal Oxides*, corroborates solid rare-earth sesquioxide structures (including Y2O3/La2O3 and the Dy–Lu sequence). Separate primary producer physical-form records corroborate CeO2, Pr6O11 and Tb4O7. Pr6O11/Tb4O7 retain the original approximate-composition caveat. These references corroborate phases only; the direct metal/oxygen reaction itself comes from the explicit WebElements equation and burning context.
- No generic oxygen-atmosphere control is introduced just because O2 is an explicit reactant. O2 remains an ingredient.
- Gaseous Br2 or I2 does **not** automatically become a sufficient Heat condition. Unless the source explicitly supplies warming/heating, these records are equation-only and preserve the vapor-form qualification.

## Withheld source claims

- **OsF7:** removed after independent reviewer corroboration of the primary reinvestigation, “Osmium(VII) Fluorine Compounds”, DOI [10.1021/ic0608290](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ic0608290). The old neutral heptafluoride preparation was not reproducible; only OsF6 was obtained.
- **TcF7:** a proposed high-valent fluoride, not established by this source-equation assertion; the theoretical paper DOI [10.1021/ic7007039](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ic7007039) discusses prospective preparation. TcF6 remains accepted.
- **EuI3:** the source itself flags disputed existence. Recent primary work DOI [10.1002/zaac.202500005](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/zaac.202500005) states the binary solvent-free triiodide is unknown. No Eu+I2 endpoint is invented.
- **ReF6(s):** room-phase conflict remains uncorrected; ReF7(s) and Re2O7(s) are retained.
- **Hg oxidation equation:** copied Hg(s) is inconsistent with mercury’s liquid reactant phase; withheld pending complete independent phase/condition correction. Its four explicit Hg(l) halogen equations are retained as equation-only.
- **Te8 halide rows / TeF6(l):** suspect bulk tellurium form and hexafluoride phase are quarantined. TeO2 remains. More sources can repair these gaps later; this is not a claim tellurium cannot form halides.
- **Pd mixed fluoride/bracket form, PtF5 tetramer, malformed Pd+CBr2 row, TlCl3/TlBr3 and WBr6:** omitted in this pass pending identity/phase/condition harmonization. This is conservative scope management rather than a no-reaction finding.

Independent review additionally corroborated high-fluoride identities using primary Drews et al., *Inorganic Chemistry* 2006, DOI 10.1021/ic052029f (nine MF6 crystal structures); its low-temperature structures are not treated as room-temperature kinetic proof. Full conditions are still incomplete for most noble-metal halide records, so they remain equation-only.

## Validation and limits

- All 150 records pass positive-integer coefficient, exact elemental conservation, neutral-charge and exact-two-distinct-element reactant checks.
- Production `validateLibrary` run on the standalone candidate returns **zero errors**.
- All source references resolve within the candidate; retained source artifact checksums match.
- The reproduction helper `raw/metal-binary/build-metal.py` uses an explicit reviewed allowlist. SymPy balances already specified products and does not predict them. Its duplicate comparison is the baseline public snapshot; rerunning after integration can intentionally omit already-merged records.

Conditions and effects are deliberately qualitative. A selected product is not asserted as the exclusive outcome for every allotrope, phase, ratio or temperature. Equation-only records display source chemistry but cannot animate an unsupported room-temperature reaction. No hazardous preparation quantities or optimization instructions are added. Source checks and AI review do not constitute external teacher approval; that remains pending.
