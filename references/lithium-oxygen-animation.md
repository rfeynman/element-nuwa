# Lithium–oxygen animation correction

The two lithium/oxygen records previously had only the solid effect. Generic solid products also used the crystal graphic associated visually with ice. No actual cooling effect was assigned, but the display was misleading and omitted combustion.

Both the principal Li2O and minor Li2O2 combustion records now include heat, flame and solid, with the red flame observation independently supported by [RSC Education](https://edu.rsc.org/experiments/heating-group-1-metals-in-air-and-in-chlorine/732.article). [WebElements](https://www.webelements.com/lithium/chemistry.html) supports white oxide as principal product and white peroxide as a minor coproduct. Equations, ignition conditions, and the non-exclusive-product qualifications are preserved. Source links and retrieval date accompany the records. No laboratory quantities or instructions are added.

Solid products use deposits/powder rather than ice-shaped crystals. Ice/snow effects require cooling; the tall crystal graphic requires an explicitly freezing scenario. Red flame matching uses the explicit observation, not a generic lithium-ion assumption.

Curated binary input and merger version are updated reproducibly. Snapshot .6 has 433 entries and 129 sources; conservation validation passes. Regression checks render both actual lithium records and require red fire, heat and deposit, while forbidding snow, ice, frost and cooling. A separate test ensures an ordinary solid-only record never receives freezing imagery.

Published in the 1.5.4 release.
