The original omission of Al/O arose because the WebElements equation writes O2(l). Rejecting that source typo without finding an independent equation omitted a standard reaction. Texas Tech’s Chemical Equations PDF, page14 (PDF index13), explicitly gives 4Al(s)+3O2(g)→2Al2O3(s). WebElements prose supports combustion with white light and oxide-film passivation. These independently distinguish the gas-phase equation, heated combustion, and limited room-condition surface-film formation. No source liquid-oxygen claim was silently accepted.

The scanned Grade11 Chemistry book, printed page72 / PDF page80, was visually inspected: heat arrows explicitly support Al+Cl2→AlCl3, Al+S→Al2S3, Al+N2→AlN, Al+C→Al4C3, with state labels. Its general paragraph describes heated halogen combinations. Sulfur’s S atom notation is normalized to the catalogue’s S8(s) with adjusted integer coefficients. Al2Br6(s) and Br2(l) are retained from the explicit WebElements equation. The primary Materials nitride study and Glasgow/RSC carbide study corroborate direct syntheses; the EPA advisory supports energetic Al/S behavior. No procedural temperatures, amounts, or optimization instructions are published.

The RSC/Nuffield Al/I example explicitly requires water catalysis, so it remains equation-only under current controls. The official OCR fluorine question establishes direct combination and a solid fluoride but does not supply full activation/surface conditions: equation-only. The FCC question compilation was inspected as corroboration only; its uncorroborated direct heated-AlH3 claim is excluded, rather than propagating a textbook mistake.

The authored Patnaik Handbook of Inorganic Chemicals (McGraw-Hill2003, pp3–4,392–394) supports reported heated Al/P and Al/Se products and heated In2O3 synthesis. AlP/Al2Se3 remain equation-only because allotrope/complete form-specific conditions are missing. Their P(s)/Se(s) tokens explicitly retain the source’s unspecified allotropes; Se is not silently rewritten as molecular Se8. In2O3 has a qualitative heat scenario, with solid state supported by its powder description. The primary gallium study supplies a liquidGa/O2 oxidation equation and native-oxide context; it is equation-only, not a claim of unrestricted bulk conversion or nitride synthesis.

Only factual equations and original short teaching explanations are served; source books, artwork, scanned pages and laboratory procedures are excluded from public assets.
